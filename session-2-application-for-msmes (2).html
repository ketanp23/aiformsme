      07 — Framework 3: Use, Buy or Build?

      The honest answer for almost every micro-enterprise: just use what already exists.

      Vendors will try to sell you custom "AI solutions". You rarely need them. Here's how to tell which path fits — and why "Use" wins nine times out of ten for a business your size.

      Use Start here

      Adopt ready-made tools as they are — ChatGPT, Claude, Canva, Google tools, WhatsApp Business.

      • ₹0 to a few hundred rupees a month
      • Live today, no setup or developer
      • Covers 90% of what an MSME needs
      • Risk is tiny — try, keep or drop

      Buy

      Pay for a specialised tool that fits one clear, recurring need — accounting with AI, an inventory app, a CRM.

      • Worth it once a task is daily and painful
      • Check it works in your language & on mobile
      • Prefer monthly plans; avoid lock-in
      • Ask a peer who already uses it first

      Build

      Commission something custom for your business. For micro-enterprises, almost never the right first move.

      • High cost, long wait, needs maintenance
      • Only if your edge truly depends on it
      • Prove the need with "Use" tools first
      • Revisit only after real scale
